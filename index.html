Research Interests

Non-adiabatic Dynamics

Develop fast and accurate mixed quantum-classical and semi-classical dynamics methods, and combine them with the on-the-fly dynamics to treat the non-adiabatic processes for the realistic complex molecular and material systems.

Bioluminescence and Chemiluminescence

Investigte the mechanism of virious bioluminescence and chemiluminescence processes with theoretical and computational methods.

Cavity Quantum Electrodynamics

Study the chemical reactions, charge and energy transfer processes for the molecules under light–matter coupling inside the optical cavity.
